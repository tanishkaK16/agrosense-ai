## Full Description (max 4000 characters)
AgroSense is a simple, visual crop care companion created for Indian farmers.

Know your crop health before problems spread. AgroSense monitors your fields and gives you one clear advice sentence every day. No complicated satellite maps, no confusing graphs, and no technical jargon.

### Built for Every Farmer
- One-touch voice readout: Tap the speaker button to hear any screen read aloud in your language.
- Speak in your words: Tap the microphone and say simple commands in English, Hindi, or Marathi.
- Works without internet: Saves your last known field condition so you can view your crops and alerts even in airplane mode.
- High-contrast sunlight design: Large text, warm high-contrast colors, and adjustable text sizes so you can read your screen under bright midday sun.
- Alerts on your phone: View critical water and pest warnings in the app or receive short SMS alerts on your registered mobile number.

### Plain Crop Care
- Field Health: Three simple meters for Crop Health, Soil Water, and Pest Risk.
- Action for Today: Clear advice on whether to irrigate, spray, or let the field rest.
- Easy Field Pinning: Add your field in seconds by dropping a pin on your village map.
- Multiple Crops Supported: Wheat, Rice, Cotton, Sugarcane, Soybean, and more.

AgroSense respects your privacy and works reliably in rural conditions with low network connectivity.
